BAYU AJI SUSETYOYOGYAKARTA // ID
“Every frame has a story. Every moment has a feeling. My job is to make it unforgettable.”

I am a digital creative, visual storyteller, and director of photography based in Yogyakarta. My practice bridges cinematic videography, portraiture, documentary photography, and rhythmic post-production.

With an editorial eye shaped by years behind Sony Alpha and Fujifilm systems, I approach every shoot as a deliberate emotional canvas. Light, silence, shadow, and cadence are my tools to preserve moments that resonate long after the screen fades to black.

From event after-movies and festival recaps to intimate documentary portraits and brand visual identities, I lead projects from initial storyboarding through precision DaVinci Resolve color grading.
